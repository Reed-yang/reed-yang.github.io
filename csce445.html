Why I'm Taking CSCE 445/656

This course on Computers and New Media connects directly to my work—understanding interactive narratives helps me build better controllable video generation systems, while learning about media interfaces informs how I design tools that users can actually use. As AI-generated content grows, the intellectual property discussions will be invaluable for responsible research.

Challenges in Media Tools

🎨 Creative Software Learning Curve

While basic video editing and cutting are relatively straightforward, tools that combine creative expression with aesthetic considerations—such as visual effects software (After Effects) and design tools (Figma)—present a steep learning curve. The challenge lies in mastering both the technical interface and developing the artistic sensibility required to use these tools effectively. The gap between understanding what's possible and being able to execute a creative vision remains significant.

🤖 AI-Generated Content Controllability

As someone researching generative models, I've observed firsthand that while AI-generated content has achieved initial usability, its controllability remains limited. Users can generate impressive outputs, but fine-grained control over specific aspects—whether it's maintaining identity consistency across frames, precise motion control, or adhering to specific aesthetic constraints—is still an open research challenge. This gap between what users want to create and what AI systems can reliably produce represents a fundamental difficulty in current content generation pipelines.
